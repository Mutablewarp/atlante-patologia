## Definition and epidemiology

**What is an inherited cancer syndrome?** The responsible germline mutation usually hits a DNA repair gene or a tumor suppressor gene.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c001--> Inheritance passes on a strong predisposition to cancer, not a tumor.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c002--> Inherited mutations account for roughly 1% of human cancers.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c003-->

**Which syndromes belong here?**
- Many endocrine tumors, Wilms tumor and retinoblastoma are inherited malignant tumors.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c004-->
- Some inherited tumors, such as those linked to APC, may stay benign or may turn malignant.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c005-->
- People with Bloom syndrome or ataxia-telangiectasia face a high risk of malignant tumors.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c006-->
- Germline changes in BRCA1 or in VHL each produce their own tumor syndrome.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c007-->
- Tuberous sclerosis and neurofibromatosis are phakomatoses: they disturb development, and their tumors arise chiefly in the nervous system.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c008-->

## Microscopic morphology

**What happens in the cells of these tumors?** Carriers are heterozygous in the germline, with one mutated tumor suppressor allele.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c009--> In a carrier's tumor both copies of the suppressor gene are out of action: loss of heterozygosity disables the normal copy.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c010--> PTEN is frequently decreased across many different cancers.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c011--> Yet benign hamartomas are the main tumors of Cowden syndrome, which results from germline PTEN loss.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c012-->

## Diagnosis, key lab and imaging

**When should an inherited syndrome be suspected?** Some cancer types clearly cluster in families, although mendelian inheritance explains only a few cancers.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c013--> The same tumor type is often two- to threefold more likely to develop in a patient's relatives.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c014--> Among familial cancers, those of the breast and colon stand out.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c015-->

## Differential diagnosis

**How do these conditions differ?**

| Entity | Discriminating features | Key test |
|---|---|---|
| Inherited cancer syndrome | Heterozygous germline; both alleles inactivated in the tumor.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c010--> | Germline genotype |
| Sporadic cancer | Common acquired changes impair tumor suppression; inherited defects are rare.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c017--> | Germline genotype |
| Familial clustering | Relatives at two- to threefold risk, without clear mendelian inheritance.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c014--> | Family history |
| Phakomatosis | Developmental and neoplastic features, mostly nervous system tumors.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c008--> | Clinical features |

## High-yield summary

- Inherited syndromes cause only about **1%** of cancers.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c003-->
- The genes are mostly **tumor suppressor** and **DNA repair** genes.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c001-->
- Carriers are **heterozygous**; the tumor loses the second allele (**LOH**).<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c010-->
- **Cowden syndrome**, from germline PTEN loss, yields mostly benign hamartomas.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c012-->
- Family history raises risk two- to threefold, most for **breast** and **colon** cancer.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c015-->

## Common misconceptions and exam traps

**Is the cancer itself inherited?** No. Susceptibility is inherited, and the tumor appears after the second allele is lost.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c002-->

**Does the gene predict which tumor appears?** Not always. Benign hamartomas are the main tumors of Cowden syndrome, which results from germline PTEN loss.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c012-->

**Why does BRCA1 affect mainly breast and ovary?** Women are affected more than men by germline BRCA1 mutations, and the tumors are chiefly breast and ovarian cancers.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c018--> The reason is unknown.<!--c:inherited-cancer-syndromes-c019-->
