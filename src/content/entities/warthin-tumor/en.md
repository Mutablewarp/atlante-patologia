## Definition and epidemiology

**What is a Warthin tumor?** It is a benign parotid tumor: dense lymphoid tissue holds glandular spaces that have turned cystic.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c001--> Among monomorphic adenomas, it is the most common.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c002--> Monomorphic adenomas contain no mesenchymal tissue; their epithelial cells line up regularly, most often as glands.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c003--> Of all benign salivary gland tumors, 5% to 10% are monomorphic adenomas.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c004-->

**Who gets it?** Among salivary gland tumors, it is the only one with a male predominance.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c005--> Most cases arise after age 50, and in general the tumor occurs after age 30.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c006-->

## Etiology and risk factors

**What causes it?** The reference pages for this card name no cause and no risk factor, and they do not mention smoking. Check a second source before teaching any association.

## Pathogenesis

**Where does the tumor come from?** The histogenesis is uncertain.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c007--> Parotid-region lymph nodes usually contain small islands of salivary tissue.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c008--> The tumor may start when these salivary inclusions multiply.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c009-->

## Gross morphology

**What does it look like to the naked eye?** The reference pages give no gross description, so this card does not describe one.

## Microscopic morphology

**What do you see under the microscope?** The glandular spaces are prone to cystic change and have papillary projections.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c010--> Oncocytes form the cyst wall; these epithelial cells stain eosinophilic.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c011--> Germinal centers are present in the dense lymphoid tissue around the cysts.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c012-->

**What is an oncocyte?** Mitochondria fill an oncocyte, a benign epithelial cell, so its cytoplasm looks granular by light microscopy.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c013-->

## Special stains, IHC and molecular findings

**What does the pathologist look at, and with which stain?**
- **How it is studied:** on routine H&E, look for lymphoid tissue with germinal centers around papillary cysts whose lining is made of eosinophilic oncocytes.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c014-->
- The reference pages describe no special stain, immunohistochemistry, electron microscopy or molecular test for this tumor.

## Clinical presentation

**Where does it arise, and how many tumors can there be?** It arises in the parotid gland.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c015--> In 15% of cases the tumors occur on both sides.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c016--> Several tumor foci can also occur within one gland.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c017--> The reference pages give no symptoms or signs.

## Diagnosis, key lab and imaging

**How is it diagnosed?** The reference pages cover no laboratory or imaging work-up for this tumor.

## Complications and prognosis

**Is it dangerous?** The reference calls it clearly benign.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c018--> It gives no data on recurrence or complications.

## High-yield summary

- Warthin tumor is the **most common monomorphic adenoma**.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c022-->
- Histology: **dense lymphoid tissue with germinal centers** wraps **oncocyte**-lined **papillary cysts**.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c023-->
- In 15% of cases it is **bilateral**.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c024--> It can have several foci, so it can be **multifocal** within one gland.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c025-->
- It is the only salivary gland tumor with a **male predominance**.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c026-->
- Most cases arise **after age 50**, and the tumor generally occurs **after age 30**.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c027-->

## Common misconceptions and exam traps

**Is a Warthin tumor malignant because it is full of lymphoid tissue?** No. The reference calls it clearly benign.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c028-->

**Is it always a single tumor in one gland?** No. In 15% of cases tumors occur on both sides.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c029--> Within one gland there can also be several foci.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c030-->

**Are oncocytes found only in Warthin tumor?** No. Small numbers of oncocytes are a normal finding in the thyroid, the parathyroid and other organs.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c031-->

**Is oncocytoma just another name for Warthin tumor?** No. Oncocytoma is a distinct rare adenoma, and its oncocytes are arranged in cords or nests.<!--c:warthin-tumor-c032-->
