## Definition and epidemiology

**What are testicular sex cord–stromal tumors?** In these testicular neoplasms, the tumor cells imitate either Sertoli cells or Leydig cells.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c001--> Together they account for about 5% of testicular tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c002--> The interstitial cells of the testis are the model for the rare Leydig cell tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c003--> Leydig cell tumors have two age peaks, one in childhood and one between the third and sixth decades, though they can arise at any age.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c004--> The other main group, Sertoli cell tumors, is defined by neoplastic Sertoli cells.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c005-->

## Etiology and risk factors

**What causes these tumors?** The pages used for this card name no cause or risk factor.

## Pathogenesis

**How do these tumors cause symptoms?** Hormone production by a Leydig cell tumor may be androgenic, estrogenic or both.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c006-->

## Gross morphology

**What do they look like on the cut surface?** Some Leydig cell tumors look encapsulated; in general they are sharply demarcated and measure 1 to 10 cm.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c007--> On section, Leydig cell tumors range from yellow to brown.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c008--> The lobulated look of the bigger ones comes from fibrous bands running through them.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c009--> A Sertoli cell tumor measures only 1 to 3 cm, has clear edges, and cuts yellow-gray.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c010-->

## Microscopic morphology

**What do the cells look like?** The cytoplasm of Leydig tumor cells is abundant, eosinophilic or vacuolated, and the nuclei are round and uniform.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c011--> Reinke crystals, typical of normal Leydig cells, are rectangular bodies lying in the cytoplasm.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c012--> Reinke crystals appear in about 30% of Leydig cell tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c013--> Within a fibrous scaffold, Sertoli cell tumors form tubules or cords made of columnar cells.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c014--> A basement membrane wraps the tubules, which look like seminiferous tubules without germ cells.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c015--> Malignant Sertoli tumors are rare; they form few tubules or cords, and the cells vary in shape with patchy necrosis.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c016-->

## Special stains, IHC and molecular findings

**Which stains or tests help?** The pages used for this card describe no special stain, immunohistochemical or molecular test; the diagnosis rests on the H&E features above.

## Clinical presentation

**How do patients present?** A prepubertal boy with a Leydig cell tumor matures early in body and sexuality because of the androgens.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c017--> Certain adults with a Leydig cell tumor show gynecomastia and feminization instead.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c018--> A scrotal mass is what brings most patients with a Sertoli cell tumor to a doctor, and most of them are under 40.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c019--> Hormonal effects seldom occur with Sertoli cell tumors, and any that do are vague.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c020-->

## Diagnosis, key lab and imaging

**Do hormone levels identify the tumor?** No characteristic hormonal pattern exists in Leydig cell tumors; levels of estrogen or testosterone can be elevated.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c021--> The pages give no imaging findings.

## Complications and prognosis

**Are these tumors dangerous?** Benign behavior is the rule, in about 90% of Leydig cell tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c022--> Predicting from the histology how a Leydig cell tumor will behave is difficult.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c023--> After orchiectomy, every child with a Leydig cell tumor is cured, as are nearly all adults.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c024--> About 90% of Sertoli cell tumors are also benign.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c025--> Orchiectomy is curative for them.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c026-->

## High-yield summary

- **Sex cord–stromal tumors** make up about 5% of testicular tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c030-->
- **Reinke crystals** are typical of Leydig cells and appear in some Leydig cell tumors.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c031-->
- A Leydig cell tumor can cause **precocious puberty** in boys and **gynecomastia** in adults.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c032-->
- A **Sertoli cell tumor** forms tubules or cords and usually presents as a scrotal mass in patients under 40.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c033-->
- About **90%** of both types are **benign**, and **orchiectomy** is curative.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c034-->

## Common misconceptions and exam traps

**Does a bland-looking Leydig cell tumor prove it is benign?** No. Histology predicts behavior poorly.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c035-->

**Do all Leydig cell tumors contain Reinke crystals?** No. Only about 30% do.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c036-->

**Do Sertoli cell tumors usually cause an endocrine syndrome?** No. They usually present as a scrotal mass, with few or vague hormonal effects.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c037-->

**Is a testicular tumor in a man always malignant?** Not here: about 90% of Leydig and Sertoli tumors are benign.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c038-->

## Mnemonics

- **Leydig = L, R:** Lobulated and yellow-brown, with Reinke crystals.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c039-->
- **Sertoli = S, S, S, T:** Small, Solid, Scrotal mass, Tubules.<!--c:testicular-sex-cord-stromal-tumors-c040-->
